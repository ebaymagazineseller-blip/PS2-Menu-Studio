Get a double-click PS2MenuStudio.exe (no Python, no installing anything)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Make a free account at github.com and click New repository (any name).
2. On the new repo page click "uploading an existing file" and drag in
   EVERYTHING from this folder (including the .github folder). Click Commit.
3. Open the Actions tab -> "Build Windows app" -> wait ~2 minutes for the green check.
4. Click the finished run, download "PS2MenuStudio-Windows" at the bottom, unzip it.
5. Double-click PS2MenuStudio.exe. (Windows may say "unknown publisher":
   click More info -> Run anyway. That's normal for unsigned apps.)
